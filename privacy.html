TabGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21 · Applies to TabGuard Chrome Extension v0.2.1 and later

Summary. TabGuard stores your tab sessions locally on your device. The only third-party service it contacts is ExtensionPay, used only if you choose to purchase the Pro plan. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

1. What TabGuard stores locally

All session data is stored in IndexedDB (tabguard_db) on your device:

  • Tab URLs, titles, pinned status, window index — only for sessions YOU explicitly save
  • Session labels you type
  • A SHA-256 checksum used to verify integrity on restore
  • Timestamps and your local preferences

This local data never leaves your device.

2. Third-party service: ExtensionPay

TabGuard uses ExtensionPay only if you choose to purchase the Pro plan:

  • ExtensionPay processes your payment via Stripe. TabGuard never sees or stores your card details.
  • ExtensionPay stores the email address you provide at checkout, used solely to issue and restore your license.
  • TabGuard fetches your license status from extensionpay.com to unlock Pro features.
  • ExtensionPay privacy policy: https://extensionpay.com/privacy

If you never purchase Pro, no payment or personal data is transmitted to ExtensionPay.

3. What TabGuard does NOT do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No reading of page content — only tab metadata (URL, title, pinned flag)
  • No selling or sharing of any data
  • No account system of its own
  • No cookies

4. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhy
tabsRead tab URL/title to save the sessions you request
storageSave preferences and session data
unlimitedStorageIndexedDB quota for many saved sessions
alarmsSchedule the 30-minute auto-backup
host_permissions: https://extensionpay.com/*Only to verify your Pro license with ExtensionPay

5. Data deletion

  • Delete any individual session from the popup (Delete button).
  • Remove all local data by uninstalling TabGuard, or via Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data → IndexedDB for chrome-extension.
  • To delete your ExtensionPay license record, email the address below and it will be removed.

6. Children's privacy

TabGuard is not directed at children under 13. No personal data is collected by TabGuard itself.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will ship with the extension release and be posted at this URL.
